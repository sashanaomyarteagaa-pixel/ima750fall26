Every hero needs a Cape (2023)

A little boy finds himself with only the company of his stuffed toys. Bored and with no one to pay attention to him he takes it upon himself to escape into a world where he is the hero of his environment. The story highlights the creativity and emotional depth of childhood, especially in moments of isolation. I took on multiple roles in this project, which pushed me to step outside of my comfort zone. It was one of the first times I actively advocated for myself in a production setting and embraced new responsibilities. Although I had never worked in sound before, I committed fully to learning the role and believe I demonstrated both adaptability and dedication through the final result.

Roles: 1st Assistant Camera, Sound Mixer (Day 2), Grip (Day2)
