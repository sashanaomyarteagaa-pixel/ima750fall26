Reading 6

Chapters 1 & 2 from The Electronic Disturbance (by CAE, 1994)

The CAE calls for electronic disturbance—interventions aimed at jamming, spoofing, hacking, and subverting the digital and symbolic flows of power to shatter the illusion of total security. The reading talks about Tactics of Electronic Disturbance, mentioning that power hides inside or behind electronic and institutional bunkers; these bunkers can be corporations, state authorities, churches, shopping malls, even administrative centers. Again, bunkers can be physical, but they are also electronic, which is why resistance must also go electronic.

We as artists have seen the misuse of art and digital art as propaganda, as ways of manipulation and use for fear tactics. Art brings people together. We have seen this in post-revolutionary Russia, and the use of mass spectacles: Outdoor re-enactments staged in public squares brought people together & were highly directed by the military to rewrite historical memory. This is why the CAE is calling to action. We have experienced as a people the power that electronic bunkers have to hurt and divide us. In today's world, we are being highly surveilled even in times of vulnerability. Facial recognition, AI, and institutions not protecting our information or safety- what is keeping us from fighting back in this way? fear. Fear of knowing that they will know, that in a country run by oligarchies that have more “power” to stop electronic resistance, digital art, visual art, music, and creative writing can continue to be distributed and be uplifting to people who also want to be part of the resistance, but just need a little push. Art is the key ingredient for movement because we have seen proof of it when it's in the wrong hands
