Trying (2023)

“Trying” is a silent short film that explores pregnancy, miscarriage, and grief within a lesbian relationship. By removing dialogue, the film centers the raw emotional experiences of a queer woman navigating the vulnerability and intensity of trying to conceive. It captures cycles of hope, disappointment, and loss, while highlighting the resilience required to keep pursuing the dream of building a family with the person she loves the most. The film moves towards a moment of success and joy, offering a powerful reminder that perseverance through grief can lead to happiness and new beginnings. This project was one of the first where I intentionally began incorporating queerness into both visual storytelling and emotional narrative. Initially, I had not written the character as a queer woman, but during the shoot I realized something was missing. Improvising on set, I added a last-minute scene that not only reflected my identity as a writer and director, but also made the story more authentic and relatable to women in lesbian relationships trying to convince. Roles: Director, Writer, Cinematographer
