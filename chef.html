Chef Florence (2023)

This film follows a chef with the skills, talents, and years of experience under his belt. After overhearing a conversation from his clients that he finds distasteful, he prepares a dish that may serve as subtle revenge. The narrative plays with ambiguity, leaving the audience to question whether his actions are intentional or simply part of his creative expression. I worked as the second camera operator and worked with the Canon EOS C500 on this studio-based production, collaborating closely with the director and fellow camera operators. Together we ensured that the visual storytelling aligned with the director’s and writer’s vision. This experience strengthened my ability to work with a camera and collaborate within a team while maintaining attention to detail in shot composition and coverage.

Role: 2nd Camera
