What Can I Control? (2022)

This short documentary is about the Tisch Drama major Caterina Korhammer. What Can I Control? discovers her feelings and takes us on her journey of emotions about being in a new city and missing life back home in Australia. This is the very first short documentary that I have ever done, so it was a new concept and realm for me as a filmmaker. I was fortunate enough to direct and film alongside my crew and also incorporate questions to ask our talent that led us to the documentary’s main topic. After doing this doc, I was inspired and am very excited to start on new documentary projects that relate more to me and my immigration journey.

Roles: Director, Co-Cinematographer
