Lead In Our Bodies

A castle, I once called it! Although tall and ominous I viewed it to be beautiful Lack of change was prominent
That imagery fast gone When it started feeling nerving When my world started to crumble Like my walls, meant for preserving
Walls created to renew the ages Suffering from cheapness Even with broken walls there is no weakness
How many more years for a basic right? “We’re taking meaningful steps to comply” But when will you abide? Fixing doesn’t come from a simple swing-by
And, yes, perhaps we are still here we have never been more alive Resilience is what we are told to have but it comes back from being deprived
These walls will gather with us One day they will stop falling And I will clearly see that castle We will NOT stop calling
