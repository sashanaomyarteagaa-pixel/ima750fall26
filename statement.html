Artsit Statement

My name is Sasha Naomy Arteaga, a first-generation Indigenous, queer Ecuadorian storyteller whose work centers the resilience, intimacy, and complexity of BIPOC communities like the ones that shaped my upbringing in both Ecuador & East Harlem. As a skill developer & artist, my mediums include: Screenwriting, Directing, Production design, and digital visual art. Through coding, I have found a passion in digital media where I can combine my creative skills to create social and political work. I am a 2025 graduate of New York University with a Bachelor’s degree in Film and Television and currently an MFA student at Hunter College concentrating in Integrated Media Arts. I have spent the past year balancing artistry as a screenwriter/Production designer and educator through an accelerated teaching residency while pursuing an accelerated Master’s degree in Childhood Education, with completion in June 2026. My experiences as a first-generation student continue to deeply influence my creative voice and commitment to community-driven storytelling.
