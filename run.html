The Run.(2023)

Grappling with the loss of her track partner, a young runner navigates her emotions during her first run. This silent film captures emotional development throughout the protagonist’s process of motivation of trying to get herself back on the track. As the protagonist pushes forward, the film emphasizes the internal struggle of regaining strength and purpose. As a cinematographer, I worked with the Canon EOS C200, which was a difficult challenge at first, navigating the controls and experimenting with different types of lenses. Still, gradually I began to understand how to work it and be more comfortable with it as well. This project allowed me to continue exploring narrative storytelling through silence, focusing on visual and emotional expression rather than dialogue. It was also my first time editing my own film, which came with both challenges and discoveries. While I initially struggled, I ultimately embraced my mistakes and incorporated them into the final dive. One particular scene, where Cece recalls her partner’s encouragement, was left imperfect because it was honest and necessary to the story.

Roles: Writer, Director, Cinematographer
