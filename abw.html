ABW Yearbook Video (2024)

As a summer videographer at A Broaderway Foundation, I had creative control and captured incredible, irreplaceable moments for the future leaders. This yearbook video was shown at the camp’s end-of-summer show, and served as documentation for the camp’s social media accounts and websites. As an alumni camper coming back to my old camp and being able to record and document moments of joy was an absolute honor and brought back a lot of nostalgia. I knew I needed to document the moments of joy with these campers the way that other videographers had done with my years at ABW./p>
